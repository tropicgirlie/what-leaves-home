# What Leaves Home

A private household weigh-in for noticing what enters waste and recycling streams, trying small changes, and seeing a transparent same-pace scenario over time.

Live: [tropicgirlie.github.io/what-leaves-home](https://tropicgirlie.github.io/what-leaves-home/)

The project has its own doorway-and-outflow mark, drawn for What Leaves Home. It is not Luana's personal toucan-caju logo.

## Look

A risograph / zine aesthetic: hot pink, fluorescent orange and federal blue on warm newsprint, flat spot-colour shapes with hard offset shadows, halftone fills, Archivo Black display + Space Grotesk body + JetBrains Mono labels. The intro is a layered animated sort — three bins stamp onto the page, then a wrapper, an apple core and a flattened box drop into each, back-to-front on page load.

## What it does

- Record weekly weights for general rubbish, food and garden waste, and materials placed in recycling collection.
- Add household size and an optional baby-at-home context to make ideas more relevant.
- See annual and multi-year projections from the latest recorded week, with the calculation and assumptions shown.
- Keep a local history, choose small reduction ideas, clear local data, and export or import a JSON backup.
- Record what made a swap difficult, then review that friction beside the weekly household pattern.
- Use sourced, Ireland-aware field notes on cloth nappies, reusable period pads, detergent labels, and repair or reuse at home.
- Keep personal journal notes in the browser only; the practical guides link to their source material.
- Store all journal entries in this browser's local storage. The app has no account or backend.

## What it does not claim

The app records what a household places in collection streams. It cannot verify that a specific item was recycled, composted or sent to a particular final destination. Longer projections repeat the latest week for the selected number of years. They are scenarios, not forecasts or a complete consumption footprint.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step is needed.
